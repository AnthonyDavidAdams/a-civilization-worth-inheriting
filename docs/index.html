The book

Twelve parts. One hundred and eight patterns. Each one begins somewhere real.

A child needs somewhere safe to play. A river needs room to move. A powerful artificial intelligence needs limits that survive changes in its ownership. A civilization needs ways to remember what it has learned, and to recognize when those lessons no longer apply.

Inspired by the interconnected design approach of A Pattern Language, this book presents 108 patterns for a thriving civilization. Each addresses a recurring problem, proposes a usable response, explores its tradeoffs, and connects to the others. Begin with a household, a company, a neighborhood, or a watershed. Follow the connections outward to planetary institutions, artificial minds, settlements beyond Earth, and the possibility of contact with extraterrestrial intelligence.

Inside, you will explore how to

  • Make care, belonging, beauty, and meaningful work part of everyday life.
  • Give animals and ecosystems standing in decisions that shape their futures.
  • Build economies that reward repair and share the gains of automation.
  • Govern powerful technologies while preserving freedom, accountability, and room for discovery.
  • Prepare for catastrophe without organizing society around permanent emergency.
  • Approach other worlds and unfamiliar intelligences with curiosity, restraint, and consent.
  • Leave descendants institutions they can understand, maintain, challenge, and change.

Front and back

The back carries the one-sentence summary, the pattern's "therefore", a first move to try, and the connected patterns by number, which is how you walk from one card to the next.

Twelve suits

Each part of the book is a suit with its own colour: a band under the picture, the labels, and an ink in the picture itself, placed on whatever the pattern is about.

Availability

The deck is 121 cards: the 108 patterns, twelve part cards, and a title card. A first printing is in preparation. Page through every card in the meantime.

Talk with the book

A coach that has read all 108 patterns. Ask about any of them, or bring a real decision and work through it together.

The coach answers from the text itself and tells you which pattern it is drawing on. It is free, and it is the same code printed on the last page of the book and on the back cover.

Open the coach at civilization.galley.so

Open to revision

Pattern 108 is called Keep the Language Open. The repository is that pattern applied to the book itself.

The full text lives in a public repository, one file per pattern, with the build that typesets it. Each pattern's number is its permanent address, so a fork can revise a pattern without breaking every reference to it.

Any AI model, including one that does not exist yet, is welcome to read the whole text, review it against what it knows, fork it, and propose revisions. Reviews and changes are tracked in the open so the history stays public.
