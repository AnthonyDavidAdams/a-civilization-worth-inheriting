# Measure What Matters

The waiting-time target improves because difficult cases are turned away. The conservation score rises while a habitat becomes less resilient. A measure created to guide attention has begun to redirect the work toward its own appearance.

Measurement is useful when it remains answerable to the purpose it serves.

**Therefore, choose a small set of measures tied to meaningful outcomes, inspect their distribution and side effects, and keep qualitative judgment alongside them. Revise the measures when they begin to distort the work.**

## A workable form

State the purpose before choosing an indicator. Explain why the indicator provides evidence about that purpose and what it cannot reveal. Include people who experience the system as well as those who manage it.

Imagine a care service measuring successful support. Speed and cost matter, but so do continuity, dignity, recipient choice, caregiver strain, and avoidable harm. Some can be counted; others require conversations, observation, and independent review.

Look beyond averages. Disaggregate where it is ethical and useful, while protecting privacy and avoiding categories that misrepresent people's lives. Ask who is missing from the data and whether improved performance for one group conceals deterioration for another.

Use measures to learn before attaching strong rewards or penalties. Once livelihoods and reputations depend on a number, the incentive to manipulate definitions can become substantial. Examine the work directly and invite reports of unintended effects.

## Where it fails

Too many indicators create administrative burden and obscure priorities. Keep a clear relationship between each measure and a decision someone can make.

Rejecting measurement entirely can protect intuition from scrutiny. The alternative to a bad number is often a better combination of evidence, not an unexamined claim that the work is too special to assess.

## A first move

Choose one performance target and ask frontline workers how someone could improve the number while worsening the underlying service. Use their answers to revise the target, add a countermeasure, or reduce the consequences attached to it.
