# Entry points by scale and problem

These are starting points rather than an exhaustive taxonomy. Most patterns belong at more than one scale, and their cross-references invite movement between scales.

## By problem

**We are meeting present needs by transferring costs to others:** Enough for the Living (2), Proportionate Caution (6), Account for the Full Cost (38), A Fair Transition (45), Map Who Bears the Cost (101).

**Nobody seems responsible:** Guardians With Duties (27), Ownership With Obligations (42), Power Must Explain Itself (46), Auditable Power (65), Institutions With Successors (80).

**People cannot leave, refuse, or challenge:** Consent as a Daily Practice (13), Rights That Majorities Cannot Remove (48), The Right to Exit and Belong (49), Keep Dissent Safe (57), Freedom From Machine Persuasion (67), Settlements That Can Refuse (85).

**A useful system has become difficult to repair or replace:** Keep the Capacity to Repair (9), Materials That Can Return (31), Pay for Maintenance First (32), Human Skills Worth Keeping (68), A Commons of Useful Tools (69), No Irreversible Surrender (72).

**The economy is growing while ordinary life becomes harder:** A Floor Beneath Everyone (4), An Economy of Enough (37), Share the Gains of Invention (39), Common Wealth Dividends (40), Pay for Care (43), Time That Is Your Own (91).

**We disagree about what is happening:** Shared Reality (55), Show the Work (56), Keep Dissent Safe (57), Public Interest Science (61), Measure What Matters (63), Confirm Before Announcing (87).

**We are unprepared for interruption:** Enough Local Capacity (36), Diverse Systems (73), Failure That Stays Local (74), Reserves Before Scarcity (76), Practice the Emergency (77), Recovery Before Disaster (78), A Civilization That Can Reboot (79).

**Life feels efficient but thin:** Many Ways to Flourish (5), A Common Table (14), A Place to Be Alone (15), Beauty in Ordinary Places (33), Time That Is Your Own (91), A Living Culture (92), Wonder Without Compulsion (94), Games Worth Playing (95), Freedom to Be Strange (97), Beauty Without Possession (99).

**We have a promising idea and need a responsible way to begin:** Begin With a Place (100), Map Who Bears the Cost (101), Assemble a Working Language (102), Small Trials With Real Boundaries (103), Independent Eyes (104), Publish the Failures (105), Make Better Practices Easier (106), Return and Reconsider (107).
